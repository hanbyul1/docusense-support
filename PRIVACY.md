# Privacy Policy for DocuSense

## Overview
DocuSense respects your privacy. This app processes documents such as receipts and utility bills.

## Data Collection
DocuSense does NOT collect or store personal data on external servers.

## On-Device Processing
All document processing (OCR and analysis) is performed locally on your device.

## Third-Party Services
The app may use Firebase services for analytics and performance monitoring. These services may collect anonymized usage data.

## Data Usage
Any data processed remains on your device unless explicitly shared by the user.
